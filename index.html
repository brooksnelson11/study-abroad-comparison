Everything researched so far, on one sheet. Descriptions are bulleted with links to sources. Rows start collapsed — expand one, or use the toggle to open/close all at once. Shared for Brooks + his surfing friend — general-elective-only criteria, no business/finance credit path required.
Match Score = equal-weighted average of Fishing Fit, Nature (×20), College Scene (×20), Cost (inverted, cheaper = higher), Safety (Good=100 / Watch=55 / Flag=15). Surf Fit, Travel Access, and Public Transit are separate columns, NOT folded into Match Score — every column is independently sortable, click any header to re-rank by just that one thing. Fishing Fit and Surf Fit = % of your Jan–May window inside the real, sourced good season for that activity. Travel Access = how cheap/easy it is to reach OTHER interesting places from here (e.g. Granada's cheap Spain/Europe rail+flight access vs. New Zealand's isolation) — this is deliberately separate from local Transit, which only covers getting around the city itself. Nature, College Scene, and Travel Access are judgment calls based on the research, not sourced per-claim like Fishing/Surf Fit. Check the box on any row to add it to the Compare tab above.
